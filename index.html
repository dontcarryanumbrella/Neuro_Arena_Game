Portal

Worlds reached through the gate. Any visible destination can be entered; some are far deadlier than others. Your fighter fights every room; between rooms you coach it.

How worlds work

A world is a line of rooms, then its bosses. Each room starts at full health; lose one and retry it. Clearing a room for the first time pays part of the world's exploration jade; a boss's first defeat breaks one of the lid's seals, unlocks its Transcendence finale and pays a large amount of jade. Running a world again pays its foes' bounties and a quarter of the exploration jade.

Only kills in the worlds raise your level. Training pays a tenth of the bounty; battles, sparring and trials pay nothing.

Memory Pavilion

A room that reconstructs what you have met. Fights here are memories: they train the brain and teach techniques half again faster than the worlds, but they feed nothing and pay almost nothing.

Levels

Every different foe or boss your fighter fells in the worlds (summoned creatures don't count) raises its level by one. Training, battles, sparring and trials don't: they are only memories. Each level adds health, energy, energy regen and posture, up to 300 health, 300 energy, 30 regen and 250 posture once every foe and boss has fallen. New stats apply from the next life.

How abilities work

Fighting a foe for long enough (all lives added up) teaches the fighter that foe's ability. Two can be equipped; they cost energy and have cooldowns. In Auto, every few lives the fighter re-picks: it keeps abilities whose lives went better than average and tries ones it hasn't used. Play keys: Q and E.

How talents work

Defeating foes earns jade ◆: bigger foes give more, bosses give 120. Kills while training earn a fifth as much as fights you watch, play or battle. Each spin draws one talent: . A purple or better is guaranteed within 30 spins. Duplicates are refunded as jade. You have 10 talent slots: common talents take 1, purple and red ones 2, black ones up to 3. Changes apply from the next fight.

Ancestral Hearth

The fire reveals lineages from the worlds in the jar. Each gives your fighter a different way to fight. One race can be equipped at a time; changes take effect in the next fight.

Same odds as talents: White 40%, Green 28%, Blue 18%, Purple 9%, Red 4%, Black 1%. Purple or better within 30 race spins. Duplicates refund jade. Unlocked races can be equipped again for free.

Learning

How the learning works

The fighter is one neural network (PPO). Every 1/15 s it reads 52 senses and picks a direction and an action. Rewards from the Rewards section score each moment. After a batch of decisions ("learn every"), it shifts toward choices that turned out better than it expected.

Curiosity keeps it trying new things. Foresight is how far ahead it values rewards. Network is neurons per hidden layer, e.g. 64,64. Changing it starts over.

Identity

Who your fighter is becoming, read from how it actually fights across its lives. You shape it by choosing opponents and rewards; you can't pick it.

How identity works

Traits drift slowly with every training life: aggression (how much it attacks), defense (guarding and parrying), mobility (moving and rolling), precision (parries, posture breaks and kills per attack), endurance (fighting on at low health, soaking damage) and versatility (using several abilities).

Awakening appears after 20 lives, shaped by the strongest trait, and only changes if another trait clearly overtakes it. Its gauge fills as the fighter deals and takes damage; when full it awakens for 8 seconds.

Ultimate Special is forged once the fighter is awakened. It is built from the two abilities it has equipped (or its two most-used, if fewer are equipped): supercharged casts of both, then a finisher that depends on its awakening. Its gauge fills as it deals damage, faster while awakened. At its last stage it adds a finale borrowed from a boss: each boss's first defeat in the worlds unlocks its finale, and you choose which one it ends with.

Controls and rules

Play: WASD move, J strike, K heavy, Space guard, L kick, Shift roll, Q / E abilities, R restart.

Keys: T train on/off, P pause.

Guard blocks but fills posture. Full posture = stunned. Guard right as a hit lands = parry. Heavy breaks blocks but can be parried. Kick goes through guard. Roll repositions (no invincibility). Hits in a row add up to +15% damage.

Enemy tells: yellow ! strike, orange ! heavy, green ! kick, red !! unblockable.
