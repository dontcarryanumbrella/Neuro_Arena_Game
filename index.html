How abilities work

Fighting a foe for long enough (all lives added up) teaches the fighter that foe's ability. Two can be equipped; they cost energy and have cooldowns. In Auto, every few lives the fighter re-picks: it keeps abilities whose lives went better than average and tries ones it hasn't used. Play keys: Q and E.

How talents work

Defeating foes earns jade ◆: bigger foes give more, bosses give 120. Kills while training earn a fifth as much as fights you watch, play or battle. Each spin draws one talent: . A purple or better is guaranteed within 30 spins. Duplicates are refunded as jade. You have 10 talent slots: common talents take 1, purple and red ones 2, black ones up to 3. Changes apply from the next fight.

Learning

How the learning works

The fighter is one neural network (PPO). Every 1/15 s it reads 52 senses and picks a direction and an action. Rewards from the Rewards section score each moment. After a batch of decisions ("learn every"), it shifts toward choices that turned out better than it expected.

Curiosity keeps it trying new things. Foresight is how far ahead it values rewards. Network is neurons per hidden layer, e.g. 64,64. Changing it starts over.

Kills

Everything your fighter (or its summons and allies) has felled, in training and in the fights you watch, play or battle. The boss it has felled most since its identity began gives its special its finale.

Identity

Who your fighter is becoming, read from how it actually fights across its lives. You shape it by choosing opponents and rewards; you can't pick it.

How identity works

Traits drift slowly with every training life: aggression (how much it attacks), defense (guarding and parrying), mobility (moving and rolling), precision (parries, posture breaks and kills per attack), endurance (fighting on at low health, soaking damage) and versatility (using several abilities).

Awakening appears after 20 lives, shaped by the strongest trait, and only changes if another trait clearly overtakes it. Its gauge fills as the fighter deals and takes damage; when full it awakens for 8 seconds.

Ultimate Special is forged once the fighter is awakened. It is built from the two abilities it has equipped (or its two most-used, if fewer are equipped): supercharged casts of both, then a finisher that depends on its awakening. Its gauge fills as it deals damage, faster while awakened. At its last stage it adds a finale borrowed from the boss it has felled most (see Kills).

Controls and rules

Play: WASD move, J strike, K heavy, Space guard, L kick, Shift roll, Q / E abilities, R restart.

Keys: T train on/off, P pause.

Guard blocks but fills posture. Full posture = stunned. Guard right as a hit lands = parry. Heavy breaks blocks but can be parried. Kick goes through guard. Roll repositions (no invincibility). Hits in a row add up to +15% damage.

Enemy tells: yellow ! strike, orange ! heavy, green ! kick, red !! unblockable.
