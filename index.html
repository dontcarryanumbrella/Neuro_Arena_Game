Portal

Worlds reached through the gate. Any visible destination can be entered; some are far deadlier than others. You fight every room; adjust your abilities between rooms.

How worlds work

A world is a line of rooms, then its bosses. Each room starts at full health; lose one and retry it. Clearing a room for the first time pays part of the world's exploration jade; a boss's first defeat breaks one of the lid's seals, unlocks its Transcendence finale and pays a large amount of jade. Running a world again pays its foes' bounties and a quarter of the exploration jade.

Only kills in the worlds raise your level. Training pays a tenth of the bounty; battles and trials pay nothing.

Memory Pavilion

A room that reconstructs what you have met. Fights here are memories: they train the brain and teach techniques half again faster than the worlds, but they feed nothing and pay almost nothing.

Levels

Every different foe or boss you defeat in the worlds (summoned creatures do not count) raises your level by one. Training, battles and trials don't: they are only memories. Each level adds health, energy, energy regen and posture, up to 300 health, 300 energy, 30 regen and 250 posture once every foe and boss has fallen. New stats apply from the next life.

Move costs

Strike 10, heavy 22, kick 18, roll 20 energy. Guard is free but halves regen. A parry gives back 25.

Opponents

Select any number of opponents. Each wave draws from your selection; it uses different opponents when possible. Changes apply to the next wave. If none are selected, all met regular foes are used.

How abilities work

Fighting a foe for long enough across your lives teaches you that foe's ability. You can equip two; they cost energy and have cooldowns. In Auto, your abilities are reconsidered every few lives, keeping those that worked and trying others. Play keys: Q and E.

How talents work

Defeating foes earns jade ◆: bigger foes give more, bosses give 120. Kills while training earn a fifth as much as fights you watch, play or battle. Each spin draws one talent: . A purple or better is guaranteed within 30 spins. Duplicates are refunded as jade. You have 10 talent slots: common talents take 1, purple and red ones 2, black ones up to 3. Changes apply from the next fight.

Learning

How the learning works

Your neural network (PPO) reads 52 senses and picks a direction and action every 1/15 second. Rewards score each moment. After a batch of decisions ("learn every"), you shift toward choices that worked better than expected.

Curiosity keeps you trying new things. Foresight is how far ahead rewards matter. Network is neurons per hidden layer, e.g. 64,64. Changing it starts over.

Identity

Who you are becoming, shaped by how you fight across your lives and by your opponents and rewards.

Choose any learned ability other than the special’s first two, or leave it on Automatic.

How identity works

Traits drift slowly with every training life: aggression (how much you attack), defense (guarding and parrying), mobility (moving and rolling), precision (parries, posture breaks and kills per attack), endurance (fighting on at low health, soaking damage) and versatility (using several abilities).

Awakening appears after 2,000 training lives, shaped by the strongest trait, and only changes if another trait clearly overtakes it. Your gauge fills as you deal and take damage; when full you awaken for 8 seconds.

Ultimate Special is forged once you awaken. It uses your two equipped abilities (or your two most-used, if fewer are equipped), followed by a finisher shaped by your awakening. Your gauge fills as you deal damage, faster while awakened. At the last stage, choose a finale borrowed from a defeated world boss.

Controls and rules

Play: WASD move, J strike, K heavy, Space guard, L kick, Shift roll, Q / E abilities, R restart.

Keys: T train on/off, P pause.

Guard blocks but fills posture. Full posture = stunned. Guard right as a hit lands = parry. Heavy breaks blocks but can be parried. Kick goes through guard. Roll repositions (no invincibility). Hits in a row add up to +15% damage.

Enemy tells: yellow ! strike, orange ! heavy, green ! kick, red !! unblockable.
